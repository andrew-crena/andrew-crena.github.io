How it works

  • Stream: turntable → USB audio interface → BUTT (AAC, 256 kbps) → Icecast → a small Python service → ngrok. The player tries two addresses and two formats, and reconnects if the audio drops.
  • What's playing: while someone is here, the service sends Shazam about 13 seconds of the stream every 20 seconds (via the open-source shazamio library) and matches the answer to my Discogs collection, so the card shows the actual pressing. When no one is here, it asks nothing.
  • When Shazam misses: I set the song on the docent page, and the service follows the rest of that side by the Discogs track lengths.
  • Letter book: each song links to memories listeners have written about it, its album or its artist. Letters appear once I've read them.
  • Privacy: no accounts, no analytics. YouTube loads only if you press Watch.

The audio is quiet between records; if it can't connect, the set has probably ended. Headphones near the room may hear the speakers slightly ahead of the stream. Open the audio directly · Video on YouTube
